## Checklist

- `Instructor guide` pokryvaet `100%` aktualnogo `slide script`
- V guide est delivery profile i teaching paths dlya nuzhnoy auditorii
- V guide est rabochiy tayming dlya `Short / Standard / Deep`
- Est otdelnyy `lecturer questions bank`
- V `lecturer questions bank` est granitsy interpretatsii i sluchai dlya eskalatsii
- Yasno, chto lektor obyazatelno dolzhen skazat i chto ne dolzhen obeshchat
- Dlya `worker` track kurs ponyaten bez metodista ryadom
- Dlya `ITR` track raskryty organizatsionnye i upravlencheskie voprosy
- Vizualy dostatochny dlya obyasneniya, a ne tolko dlya ukrasheniya
- Otkrytye tekhnicheskie probely zazemleny v ogranicheniyakh kursa
