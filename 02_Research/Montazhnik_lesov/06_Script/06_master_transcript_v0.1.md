## Conflict Check

1. transcript ne dolzhen dubliruyut slayd bukvalno, esli smysl mozhno peredat segmentom;
2. safety-smysl ne dolzhen zhit tolko v speaker notes;
3. asset-bound tekst ne schitaetsya reshennym tolko potomu, chto est kartinka;
4. `on-screen`, `voiceover` i `reference` ne dolzhny slivat'sya v odno pole;
5. multilingual-sloy ne dolzhen zamedlyat vypusk bazovogo `ru` release.

## Svyaz s maintenance

- Kakie izmeneniya v transcript schitat `patch / minor / major`: wording i glossary utochneniya = `patch`; peresborka checkpoint wording = `minor`; novaya segmentatsiya ili izmenenie route relevance = `major`
- Kogda nuzhno obnovlyat vse language packs: kogda menyaetsya `voiceover master text`, glossary-lock ili segment ID
- Kogda nuzhno pereozvuchivat audio: kogda menyaetsya smysl segmenta, a ne tolko reference note
- Kak pometchat rassinkhron mezhdu `slide script`, `narration pack` i `master transcript`: cherez `Stage 13` kak `version drift / multilingual signal`
