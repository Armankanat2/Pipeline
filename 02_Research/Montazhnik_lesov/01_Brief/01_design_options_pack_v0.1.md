# Design Options Pack

## Status

Starter draft for `10` design concepts.

## Obshchaya informatsiya

- Nazvanie kursa: `Montazhnik lesov`
- Kod kursa: `ML`
- Dlya kakogo klienta ili tipa klientov: multi-client / white-label production
- Data sborki: `2026-05-12`
- Owner: vy
- Kolichestvo kontseptov v pakete: `10`
- Rekomenduemyy generic-kontsept po umolchaniyu: `D-06 Corporate Neutral`

## Pravila ispolzovaniya

- Klient vybiraet `1` bazovyy kontsept do starta sborki finalnogo deck.
- Na etom etape my ne pererisovyvaem metodiku pod kontsept, a menyaem tolko visual shell.
- Posle vybora kontsepta mozhno primenyat branded adaptation: logo, tsveta, nazvanie klienta, title-only override.
- Esli klient ne delaet vybor, bazovym schitaetsya `D-06 Corporate Neutral`.

## Preview minimum dlya kazhdogo kontsepta

Kazhdyy kontsept v finalnom pack dlya pokaza klientu dolzhen imet minimum:

- cover slide
- content slide
- test slide
- final slide

Chtoby klient sravnival kontsepty chestno, dlya vsekh `10` variantov nuzhno pokazyvat odin i tot zhe nabor preview-slaydov.

### Preview-01 Cover

- Pokazyvaet: ton, tipografiku, cover-shell, logo-slot
- Dlya `Montazhnik lesov`: ispolzovat rabochee nazvanie kursa i odin i tot zhe podzagolovok vo vsekh kontseptakh

### Preview-02 Content

- Pokazyvaet: kak vyglyadit obychnyy obuchayushchiy slayd
- Dlya `Montazhnik lesov`: brat odnu i tu zhe temu, naprimer `ustoychivost = osnova + geometriya + kreplenie`

### Preview-03 Test

- Pokazyvaet: kak vyglyadit vopros s `4` variantami otveta
- Dlya `Montazhnik lesov`: brat odin i tot zhe testovyy vopros iz modulia `03` ili `06`

### Preview-04 Final

- Pokazyvaet: kak kontsept zakryvaet kurs i rabotaet s finalnym takeaway
- Dlya `Montazhnik lesov`: brat odin i tot zhe finalnyy posyl pro proverku i bezopasnuyu ekspluatatsiyu

## Chto klient sravnivaet mezhdu kontseptami

- obshchee vpechatlenie
- chitaemost na kontentnom slayde
- ponyatnost testovogo slayda
- sovmestimost s budushchim logo i tsvetami zakazchika
- naskolko kontsept podkhodit pod kurs dlya rabochikh i TB-tematiku

## Post-selection handoff

Posle vybora kontsepta dizayner dolzhen poluchit ne tolko kod `D-0X`, no i production-bazu dlya sborki.

- Vybrannyy kontsept: poka ne vybran
- Chto schitaetsya bazovym visual shell: budet zafiksirovano v `design system spec`
- Kakie tipograficheskie pravila zakrepleny: budet zafiksirovano posle vybora
- Kakie setki i otstupy ispolzuyutsya: budet zafiksirovano posle vybora
- Kak vyglyadyat tablitsy, skhemy i testovye slaydy: nuzhno zakrepit posle vybora
- Kakie bloki obyazatelno imeyut branded-slot: cover, final, metadata-slots
- Kak v etom kontsepte razmeshchayutsya QR / direct-link pair: nuzhno zakrepit v `design system spec`
- Kak kontsept adaptiruetsya v `A3` i `A5`: nuzhno zakrepit v `print production spec`
- Nuzhen li otdelnyy `design system spec` posle vybora: `yes`

- Content: tema `Ustoychivost lesov: osnova + geometriya + kreplenie`

## Paket kontseptov

### D-01 Industrial Grid

- Kharakter: strogo, tekhnichno, mnogo setki i konstruktivnoy geometrii
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: promyshlennym i B2B-klientam
- Preview-01 Cover: setka, konstruktsionnye linii, strogoe cover-reshenie
- Preview-02 Content: skhema lesov s tekhnicheskim podpisyvaniem
- Preview-03 Test: strogo strukturirovannyy blok s ravnym vesom variantov
- Preview-04 Final: kompaktnaya finalnaya tekhnicheskaya ramka
- Risk: mozhet pokazat'sya slishkom kholodnym dlya prostoy auditorii

### D-02 Safety Signal

- Kharakter: kontrast, TB-aktsenty, signalnaya logika
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: kursam s silnym fokusom na risk, zapret i disciplinu
- Preview-01 Cover: signalnye aktsenty i silnyy TB-ton
- Preview-02 Content: risk-point kartochki i warning-vydeleniya
- Preview-03 Test: kontrastnyy testovyy blok s khorosho vidnym voprosom
- Preview-04 Final: final s signalnym takeaway i disciplinoy deystviya
- Risk: nuzhno ne peregruzit signalnymi tsvetami

### D-03 Blueprint Clean

- Kharakter: chertezhnaya estetika, svetlyy fon, skhemy i tonkie linii
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: kursam, gde nuzhno mnogo pokazyvat konstruktsiyu i poryadok sborki
- Preview-01 Cover: chistaya chertezhnaya oblozhka
- Preview-02 Content: line-diagram po ustoychivosti ili poryadku sborki
- Preview-03 Test: akuratnyy svetlyy testovyy layout
- Preview-04 Final: finalnyy algoritm v logiche skhemy
- Risk: pri slabykh vizualakh mozhet vyglyadet slishkom pustym

### D-04 Field Manual

- Kharakter: praktichnyy instruktazh, operativnaya podacha, malo dekora
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: rabochim kursam i polevym instruktsiyam
- Preview-01 Cover: funkcionalnaya oblozhka bez izbytochnogo dekora
- Preview-02 Content: prakticheskiy slayd v logike instruktsii
- Preview-03 Test: maksimalno prostoy i chitaemyy test
- Preview-04 Final: rabochiy takeaway s deystviem
- Risk: mozhet kazat'sya menee premium, zato ochen funktsionalen

### D-05 Photo Evidence

- Kharakter: krupnye foto i sravneniya `pravilno / nepravilno`
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: kursam s silnoy foto-bazoy
- Preview-01 Cover: cover na polevom kadre
- Preview-02 Content: sravnenie `pravilno / nepravilno` po foto
- Preview-03 Test: vopros s foto-oporoy
- Preview-04 Final: final na foto ili polevom fragmente
- Risk: bez kachestvennoy foto-bazy ne raskryvaetsya

### D-06 Corporate Neutral

- Kharakter: universalnyy corporate-shell dlya bystrogo white-label
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: generic-baze i klientam bez zhestkogo vizualnogo zaprosa
- Preview-01 Cover: neytralnaya branded-oblozhka s logo-slotom
- Preview-02 Content: universalnyy korporativnyy content slayd
- Preview-03 Test: akuratnyy testovyy blok bez smelykh effektov
- Preview-04 Final: spokoynyy final s branded-slotom
- Risk: mozhet pokazat'sya slishkom bezopasnym, no eto luchshiy fallback

### D-07 Bold Modules

- Kharakter: krupnye zagolovki, modulnye razdeliteli, silnyy ritm
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: dlinnym kursam `Standard / Deep`
- Preview-01 Cover: krupnyy zagolovok i silnaya tipografika
- Preview-02 Content: modulnyy content s yavnoy ierarkhiey
- Preview-03 Test: test s krupnym voprosom i chistym ritmom variantov
- Preview-04 Final: silnyy takeaway v modulnoy podache
- Risk: nuzhno derzhat distsiplinu tipografiki

### D-08 Icon Board

- Kharakter: kartochki, ikonki, korotkie pravila i checklist-logika
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: TB-kursam, instruktsiyam i formatam s mnogimi pravilami
- Preview-01 Cover: ikonichnaya oblozhka s kartochkami
- Preview-02 Content: slayd pravil ili checklist
- Preview-03 Test: setka variantov v logike card-layout
- Preview-04 Final: kompaktnaya itogovaya doska pravil
- Risk: nuzhny akuratnye ikonki i ne slishkom mnogo melkikh elementov

### D-09 Dark Control

- Kharakter: temnyy fon, svetlye skhemy, premium-tekhnichnyy ton
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: klientam, kotorym nuzhen bolee display-vid i prezentatsionnyy kharakter
- Preview-01 Cover: temnaya oblozhka s kontrastnym zagolovkom
- Preview-02 Content: svetlaya skhema na temnom fone
- Preview-03 Test: kontrastnyy testovyy slayd s vysokoy chitaemostyu
- Preview-04 Final: display-final s akuratnym branded-slotom
- Risk: nuzhno ochen strogo proverat chitaemost teksta i pechat v PDF

### D-10 Training Poster

- Kharakter: prostoy, zapominaemyy, legko adaptiruetsya pod worker materials
- Luchshe vsego podkhodit: svyazke `prezentatsiya + A3 + A5`
- Preview-01 Cover: prostaya zapominaemaya oblozhka
- Preview-02 Content: slayd, kotoryy legko perevesti v stend ili handout
- Preview-03 Test: ponyatnyy i ne peregruzhennyy testovyy blok
- Preview-04 Final: final, kotoryy mozhno adaptirivat v worker-poster
- Risk: nuzhno ne skatit'sya v plakat bez normalnoy prezentatsionnoy logiki
