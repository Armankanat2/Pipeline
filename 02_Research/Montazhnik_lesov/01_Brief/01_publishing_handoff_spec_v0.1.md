# Publishing Handoff Spec v0.1

## Obshchaya informatsiya

- Nazvanie kursa: `Montazhnik lesov`
- Kod kursa: `ML`
- Client code: `GENERIC`
- Data sborki: `2026-05-13`
- Owner: `vy`
- Kuda peredayotsya paket: potentsialno `publisher / print vendor / client production`

## Scope handoff

- Kakie nositeli ukhodyat vo vneshniy contour: `PPTX`, `PDF`, `A3`, `A5`, notes, pri neobkhodimosti online-handoff
- Nuzhny li redaktiruemye iskhodniki: veroyatno da
- Nuzhen li otdelnyy source bundle: da
- Nuzhna li peredacha linked-assets: da
- Nuzhen li font package ili dostatochno font list: poka minimum `font list`

## Asset rights

- Minimalnyy status: po klyuchevym finalnym assetam rights dolzhny byt `own / client-provided / licensed`
- Client approvals po brand-assets: budut nuzhny, kogda poyavitsya realnyy klient
- Licenzii / ssylki na zakupku: nuzhny po storonnim ikonkam, foto i shriftam
- Chto zapreshcheno peredavat vneshne: assets bez ponyatnogo proiskhozhdeniya ili s neproyasnennymi pravami
- Chto delat s `needs approval / blocked`: ne vypuskat v finalnyy publishing contour

## Export and formats

- Print-ready PDF: da
- Digital PDF: da
- PPTX: da
- Otdelnye eksporty dlya A3 / A5: da
- Vendor-specific preset: poka net, rabotaem ot generic preflight

## Fonts and links

- Kto utverzhdaet spisok shriftov: vnutrennyaya komanda / klient posle poyavleniya brandbook
- Mozhno li peredavat fayly shriftov: neizvestno, zafiksirovat otdelno
- Nuzhen li fallback-spisok: da
- Nuzhno li proverit vneshnie ssylki i QR pered handoff: da

## Preflight rules

- Kto otvechaet za preflight: vnutrennyy owner release
- Nuzhna li testovaya pechat: da dlya `A3` i `A5`
- Nuzhna li proverka embed/links: da
- Nuzhna li proverka release naming: da

## Reshenie po gotovnosti

- Publishing handoff spec gotov: `starter draft`
- Chto vse eshche nado dosobrat: finalnyy spisok shriftov, rights-status po assetam, vendor-specific export rules
- Kakie riski ostayutsya pered release: visual gaps, nepolnyy rights-layer, otsutstvie testovoy pechati
