Option A3

Dot Start

To zhe tekhnicheskoe soedinenie, no s maloy startovoy tochkoy u plashki. Mozhet rabotat, no dobavlyaet lishnyy mikroelement.

Vertikalnaya truba
Mozhet podoyti, esli nuzhno silnee pokazat tochku vykhoda, no dlya nas est risk pereuslozhnit callout.
Option A4

Bold Pointer

Bolee tolstaya strelka s krupnym nakonechnikom. Kontrastnaya, no uzhe nachinaet domin i rovat nad samim izobrazheniem.

Vertikalnaya truba
Silnyy vizualno, no dlya nashikh uchebnykh slaydov uzhe slishkom tyazhelyy.
