# Legal Compliance Checklist v1

## Naznachenie

Etot cheklist vklyuchaetsya tol'ko dlya kursov, v kotorykh est ssylki na zakonodatel'nuyu bazu RF.

## Minimum proverki

- Kazhdaya RF-law ssylka vedet na real'no sushchestvuyushchiy dokument.
- Nazvanie dokumenta, ego nomer i tip ne pridumany.
- U kazhdoy ssylki est ponyatnyy status istochnika: `official / trusted mirror / needs verification`.
- Dlya release-grade ssylok net neproverennykh normativnykh utverzhdeniy.
- Esli dokument izmenilsya ili utratil silu, est zapolnennaya `Legal Review Form`.
- Bez review-formy ne delalas zamena nomeratsii, ssylki ili statusa.
- Zatronutye fayly posle zamyeny proshli ruchnoy smyslovoy review.
- Esli izmeneniya doshli do release, oni zafiksirovany v `13_update_log.md`.

## Reshenie

- `pass` - ssylki provereny, mapping pri neobkhodimosti est, konfliktov net.
- `revise` - est neproverennye ssylki, nekhvataet formy ili nuzhen povtornyy review.
